Helpful moving guide

A calmer move starts
before moving day.

Use this simple checklist to prepare your home or office and help the moving team understand what needs to happen.

Plan your move ↗
01 / BEFORE

Plan early

  • Confirm your preferred moving date.
  • Share both addresses and access details.
  • Check lift bookings, parking and building rules.
  • Set aside important documents and valuables.
02 / PACKING

Pack with purpose

  • Label boxes by room and contents.
  • Wrap fragile items and mark the boxes.
  • Keep daily essentials in a separate bag.
  • Tell the crew about oversized or delicate items.
03 / MOVE DAY

Make the day smoother

  • Keep walkways clear where possible.
  • Have a contact person available.
  • Check cupboards and storage spaces before leaving.
  • Walk through the new space before unloading.
